The formula behind every number

There's no separate "points rate" published per country — it falls out of one fixed rule applied through currency conversion.

01

Convert to USD

Whatever you spend — rupees, euros, won — is converted to its US dollar value using Valve's own currency conversion, refreshed periodically against market rates.

02

Apply the fixed rate

Every qualifying purchase earns a flat 100 Points per $1.00 of that USD value. Wallet top-ups and Market trades earn nothing.

03

Rate drifts with FX

Because step 1 uses a currency rate, points per 100 rupees, pesos, or lira move as currencies move — this page recalculates live rather than hardcoding stale figures.
